# SPEC — Bonus Ledger (MVP)

## Goals

1. A team lead connects a Jira project and gets every member's monthly contribution score within 10 minutes of the first sync.
2. The service generates a bonus justification draft per member, backed by evidence (issues, story points, sprint capacity, trend versus previous months).
3. HR approves or returns justifications and exports the closed period as PDF or CSV.
4. Companies pay a monthly subscription per team. When a payment fails, sync and new periods are suspended after a grace period.

## Non-goals (MVP)

- Calculating or paying out actual bonus amounts or salaries. The output is a tier recommendation plus text.
- Integrations other than Jira Cloud (no GitLab, GitHub, Linear).
- Real-time dashboards; data refreshes on sync.
- Mobile apps and SSO/SCIM.
- Ranking individuals publicly inside the team.

## Decisions

- Modular monolith, feature-first, five bounded contexts (see `docs/bc-map.md`, `docs/adr/0001-initial-setup.md`).
- TypeScript, NestJS and PostgreSQL with Prisma, with a schema per context.
- The scoring formula is versioned (`WeightingPolicy v1`). Every score stores the policy version it used.
- Justification drafts are generated by the Claude API from structured evidence only. Raw Jira comments are never sent.
- Jira tokens are encrypted at rest. Employee data is deleted 90 days after a company cancels.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Re-running a sync for the same period produces identical `IssuesImported` data (idempotent).
- [ ] A score for a member with 0 capacity days is reported as "not scored", not as 0.
- [ ] Changing `WeightingPolicy` does not change the scores of already closed periods.
- [ ] A justification cannot be approved by the same user who drafted it.
- [ ] After `BonusPeriodClosed`, justifications and scores of that period are read-only.
- [ ] After `PaymentFailed` plus a 7-day grace period, the sync stops and a banner is shown; data stays readable.
- [ ] No module imports another module's internals (lint check is green).
